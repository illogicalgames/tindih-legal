TINDIH - Stack Slab Into Tower

Privacy Policy · Effective 17 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information ILLOGICAL SDN. BHD. (registration no. 1377023-X), trading as Illogical Games ("Illogical", "we", "us"), collects when you play TINDIH - Stack Slab Into Tower ("Tindih" or the "Game"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

Tindih comes in two versions, and this policy covers both:

  • the TikTok version, a mini game that runs inside the TikTok app. TikTok's own Privacy Policy also applies to your use of TikTok;
  • the App Store and Google Play versions, an app you install on an iPhone, iPad or Android device. Apple's or Google's own privacy policies also apply to your use of their stores.

Where something applies to only one version, this policy says so.

1. Information we collect

In both versions

Game data. Your best scores, perfect stacks, saved towers (their shapes and colours), gem balance, purchases, whether you have bought No Ads, tutorial and tip progress, and how many runs you have played since your last ad. This is kept on your device and in our cloud storage, so your progress is safe if the game is closed or the device is restarted.

Gameplay records for fair leaderboards. When a run finishes, the game sends its score together with a record of each tap in that run: when it happened, where on the screen it was, where the moving slab stopped, and the timing of that frame, along with your screen size. We use these records only to check that leaderboard scores were played genuinely. A run that fails the check is not posted, and a note of it is kept on your game account.

Technical information. When the game connects to our service providers, they process technical information such as your IP address, device type, operating system and app version, as needed to provide their service.

What we never collect. Tindih does not access your precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

In the TikTok version

Your TikTok account identifier. When you open Tindih, TikTok gives the game a one-time login code. Our server exchanges it with TikTok for an identifier that is specific to Tindih (TikTok calls it an "open ID"). We use it only to connect your game progress to your TikTok account, so it is the same on every phone. We do not receive your TikTok password, phone number, email address or contacts. Your game data is kept on your device in TikTok's storage for mini games.

Your TikTok display name, only if you choose to show it. On the leaderboards you can choose to show your TikTok display name. TikTok asks for your permission first. If you allow it, we read your display name and show it next to your scores. You can hide it again at any time, and you are then shown under a randomly generated name. If you never choose to show it, we never read it.

Purchases. Gems and No Ads are bought with TikTok Beans through TikTok's payment system. We receive the order details and whether the order was paid, so we can deliver what you bought. We never receive your card or other payment details.

Advertising. The TikTok version shows ads served by TikTok: ads between runs, and ads you choose to watch for a reward. TikTok may collect information about your device and your interaction with ads to show and measure them, as described in TikTok's Privacy Policy. We receive only summary statistics about ads, not information that identifies you.

Statistics. TikTok gives us summary statistics about the game, such as how many players started it, how long it took to load and how often players return. The TikTok version does not use any location information, or any analytics or advertising tools other than those provided by TikTok.

In the App Store and Google Play versions

A game account without sign-up. The first time you open the app, Unity Gaming Services creates an anonymous player account for you, identified by a random player ID. There is no sign-in, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number. Your game data is linked to this player ID.

Your leaderboard name. You are shown on the leaderboards under a randomly generated name, or under a name you choose yourself in the game. Names are checked against a list of offensive words.

Purchases. Gems and No Ads are bought with real money through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). The store sends the game its signed proof of each purchase: the product, the transaction or order number, the date, and a code that links the purchase to your game account without identifying you. Our server checks it with the store's signature and then delivers what you bought. We never receive your card or other payment details.

Advertising. These versions show ads between runs and ads you choose to watch for a reward. Ads are provided through Unity LevelPlay, which chooses among ad networks: currently Unity's own networks (such as Unity Ads) and Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and limit fraud, these companies may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (the Advertising ID on Android, or the IDFA on iOS if you allow tracking), your IP address and the approximate location it suggests, your device type and settings, and how you interact with ads. Whether ads are personalised to your interests depends on your choices (see section 5).

Usage statistics. These versions use Unity Analytics to understand how the game is played and improve it. It records events such as starting the game, how long you play, finished runs and their scores, saved towers, rewinds, name changes, gems spent, purchases and ads shown, together with your player ID, device model, operating system, app version and country. These statistics are not used for advertising.

2. How we use information

  • to run the game and keep your progress;
  • to show leaderboards, and to keep them fair;
  • to process purchases and deliver gems and No Ads;
  • to show ads, which keep the game free to play;
  • to understand how the game is played, fix problems and improve it;
  • to comply with the law and respond to lawful requests.

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on providing the game you asked for; our legitimate interest in keeping the game working, fair and free to play; and your consent where it is needed, for showing your TikTok display name, for personalised ads and for usage statistics.

3. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share it only with:

  • Unity Technologies, whose Unity Gaming Services host our player accounts, cloud saves, leaderboards and server code, and, in the App Store and Google Play versions, also provide Unity Analytics, Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads (Unity Privacy Policy);
  • TikTok, in the TikTok version, which provides login, storage, payments, ads and statistics for mini games (TikTok Privacy Policy);
  • Google, in the App Store and Google Play versions: Google AdMob shows ads, and Google's consent tool asks for your ad choices where required (Google Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services);
  • Apple and Google as app stores, which process your purchases in those versions (Apple Privacy Policy);
  • other players, who can see the name shown for you on the leaderboards and your leaderboard scores;
  • authorities, where we are required to by law.

These companies may process information in countries other than your own, and are required to protect it. Some laws, such as those of California, treat letting ad partners use device identifiers for personalised ads as "selling" or "sharing" personal information; you can opt out of this as described in section 5.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your game data for as long as you play, and until you ask us to delete it. Daily and weekly leaderboards reset on their own; the all-time leaderboard keeps its scores until you ask for deletion. Usage statistics are kept by Unity Analytics for a limited period and then deleted. Ad partners keep information according to their own policies.

5. Your choices and rights

In the TikTok version you can hide your TikTok display name from the leaderboards at any time in the game.

In the App Store and Google Play versions:

  • Privacy choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks before showing personalised ads and before collecting usage statistics. Anyone, anywhere, can change both answers at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. Saying no to personalised ads also tells our ad partners not to "sell" or "share" your information under US state laws. You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.
  • Tracking on iPhone and iPad. The game asks for permission before it can use your device's advertising identifier for tracking. You can change this at any time in your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Advertising ID on Android. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • No Ads. Buying No Ads stops the ads shown between runs. Ads you choose to watch for a reward are still available.

Access, correction and deletion, in both versions. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your game data by emailing admin@illogicalgames.com. Tell us which version you play and your TikTok username or the name shown for you on the leaderboards, so we can find your data. We will reply within 30 days. Deleting your data removes your saves, gems and leaderboard entries, and cannot be undone. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to some uses of your information or to complain to a data protection authority.

6. Children

Tindih is not directed at children. It is for players aged 13 and over, or 16 and over in the European Economic Area. In the TikTok version, TikTok's own age requirements also apply. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections. Your gem balance and purchases can be changed only by our server, not by the game on your device, and store purchases are delivered only after our server has checked the store's signature. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed, and significant changes will be announced in the game.
